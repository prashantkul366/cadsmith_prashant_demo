# 🧩 Sample Parts | Prompt Library

Parts generated with Text to CAD from a single text prompt. Full prompts,
3D renders and A3 drawings are in the attached `Parts.pptx`.

> things listed at the bottom before you do.

| ID | Part | Key features |
|---|---|---|
| TL3 | Shaker head adapter plate | 300 × 300 × 25 plate, 8 counterbored mounting holes, 24 M8 tapped grid holes, M12 lifting holes |
| MC2 | Footpeg mounting bracket | 120 × 55 × 6 plate, counterbored frame holes, pivot hole, lightening pocket |
| MC4 | Handlebar riser top clamp | Ø28.6 half-bore clamp block, 4 counterbored holes, fillets and chamfers |
| MC7 | Lower triple clamp (simplified) | Two fork-tube clamps and a steering-stem bore |
| RB4 | SCARA tool flange | Ø63 flange, ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 tool interface, clamped hub |
| RB6 | HTD 5M timing pulley, 24T | 24-tooth HTD 5M profile, Ø44 flanges, Ø10 H7 bore, set-screw holes |
| EB7 | Steering wheel hub adapter | Ø70 × 45, 1:10 tapered bore with keyway, six M8 tapped holes |
| MF3 | Handlebar vibration fixture pedestal | 100 × 80 × 50 block, 90° V-groove, counterbored and dowel holes |
| EV9 | Battery module end plate | 150 × 100 × 10 plate, diagonal ribs, tie-rod and lifting holes |
| EV12 | Liquid cold plate | 300 × 200 × 12 plate, internal serpentine channel, G1/4 ports |

These ten were chosen to be recognisable to this audience rather than to
be easy — they replaced an earlier set of I-beams, washers and gears after
review. The range is deliberate: a plate with a hole grid, a clamp with a
half-bore, a geometric profile (HTD 5M teeth), a tapered bore with a
keyway, a ribbed plate, and an internal channel.
